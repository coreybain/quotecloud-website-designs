Questions, answered

What is QuoteCloud quoting software?

QuoteCloud is cloud-based quoting software that gives your team everything needed to create precise, professional quotes quickly, even in complex sales environments. Templates, a drag-and-drop content library, a product catalogue and flexible price tables let you build a quote in minutes, then route it for approval, send it, and have it signed and paid online, from pricing to approval to delivery in one platform.

Can customers select optional items when reviewing a quote?

Yes. Include optional products, services and add-ons directly in the pricing table. Customers choose the options they want, adjust quantities where permitted and see totals recalculate automatically, while your sales team keeps control over the products, pricing and choices available.

Can I hide internal costs and margins from customers?

Yes. You control which pricing information is visible in the customer-facing quote. Present the final selling price clearly while internal costs, supplier pricing, margins and other sensitive details stay hidden from the client.

Can I create custom pricing formulas and apply discounts?

Yes. Custom formulas and calculations apply rules for quantities, discounts, margins, markups and other pricing logic, so quotes stay accurate without separate spreadsheets. Percentage or fixed-value discounts can be added as a column, with default values applied consistently across line items.

Can I customise the pricing table, its columns and the currency?

Yes. Control the layout, styling and presentation of pricing tables so every quote reflects your brand. Choose which columns appear, such as descriptions, quantities, unit prices, discounts, optional items and totals, and set the quote currency so the correct symbol is applied across line items, subtotals and totals.

Can quotes be approved before they reach the customer?

Yes. Configurable, multi-level approval workflows route quotes to the right people, with rules based on pricing thresholds or other conditions. Full version history and a clear audit trail mean every change is tracked and customers always receive the latest version.

Can customers sign and pay directly from the quote?

Yes. Quotes are presented as clean, interactive documents with built-in, legally binding eSignatures, and online payment through gateways such as Stripe and PayPal enables same-day signing and payment. You are notified when a quote is opened, viewed, commented on or accepted.
