Questions, answered

What is CPQ software and how does QuoteCloud help?

CPQ (Configure, Price, Quote) software makes sure every configuration is valid, every price is accurate and every quote is consistent. QuoteCloud lets your team configure products and services, apply pricing logic automatically and generate accurate, approval-ready sales documents in one connected platform, from configuration through approval to delivery and customer acceptance.

Can QuoteCloud handle complex pricing models?

Yes. QuoteCloud supports fixed, volume-based and recurring pricing, tiered, tariff-based and margin-driven structures, multi-layered price tables and pricing rules based on customer, region or product mix. Calculations happen in real time, with full visibility of the margin impact at every stage.

Can I bundle products and build contract plans?

Yes. Combine products into one selectable bundle that adds multiple price line items in a single click. Build contract service plans, such as mobile phone plans with device pricing based on contract term, and set customer-specific contract pricing with percentage discounts on specific products or fixed prices.

Can customers choose options and quantities in the quote?

Yes. Interactive price tables let prospects include optional line items and add-ons just before accepting the quote, and you can optionally let them choose the quantity they want to order. Totals update automatically, and customers can then accept, e-sign and pay online.

How do I handle complex pricing calculations?

Add a hidden spreadsheet to your document for unlimited formula calculations and connect its cell values to price table lines. Price tables also support custom formula columns, so complex line-item pricing stays accurate without manual re-keying.

Can quotes and discounts be approved before they reach the customer?

Yes. Configurable approval workflows based on rules and thresholds, including multi-level approvals and multiple possible approvers for when people are unavailable, enforce pricing compliance and discount controls. Content locking keeps control over appearance, functionality and pricing options, and version history gives a full audit trail.

Does QuoteCloud CPQ integrate with my CRM and other systems?

Yes. Connect CPQ to CRM systems for opportunity and customer data, align with ERP, finance and job management systems, and use API access for custom workflows and automation. Integrations include Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, Microsoft Dynamics, Xero, QuickBooks, Stripe and PayPal.
