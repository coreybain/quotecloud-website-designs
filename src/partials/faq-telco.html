Questions, answered

What is QuoteCloud for telecommunications companies?

QuoteCloud is quoting and proposal software for telecommunications, hosted-services and managed-service providers. Sales teams configure services, build pricing, prepare professional proposals, collaborate with technical teams, get approvals and collect electronic signatures in one platform, instead of moving between spreadsheets, Word documents and separate eSign tools.

Can QuoteCloud show recurring and once-off charges in the same quote?

Yes. Monthly service costs and upfront charges such as hardware, installation and setup sit side by side in the same quote, with clear totals for each. It is built for connectivity, voice, cloud and managed-service deals where one solution mixes several charging models.

Can pricing change with the contract term?

Yes. Price the same solution on 12, 24 or 36-month terms with the right pricing attached to each option, so customers can compare contract choices and sales teams stay consistent.

Can I quote devices, SKUs, plans and bundles together?

Yes. A product catalogue holds categories, bundles and individual SKUs. Assign devices to plans, set prices per device or contract, and combine connectivity, voice, mobile, unified communications, cloud, managed services, hardware and installation in one proposal.

Can we calculate carrier buy rates and margins without showing them to customers?

Yes. Internal costs, carrier buy rates and margin calculations live inside the quote while staying hidden from the customer, so managers see the commercial position of every deal and customers see only the right pricing.

Can sales, pre-sales and engineering work on the same proposal?

Yes. Real-time collaboration lets the salesperson shape the commercial offer while a technical specialist adds network diagrams, solution architecture and implementation details in the same document, with no versions passed back and forth.

Can customers choose options and sign online?

Yes. Optional items let customers pick upgrades, support levels or alternative configurations and see pricing update. They then accept and e-sign the proposal online, with approval workflows on your side keeping discounts and margins under control.
