Questions, answered

What is QuoteCloud document generation software?

QuoteCloud is a smart document generation platform for quotes, proposals and contracts. Instead of drafting every document from scratch, teams start from branded templates, reusable content blocks and AI-assisted text, pull customer data in from their CRM, and send consistent, professional documents much faster.

Can I create branded proposals and quotes with QuoteCloud?

Yes. Customisable templates, themes and style controls give every document a unified, company-wide look, with the right layout blocks in the right places. Start from premade templates covering popular industries and document types, or create your own custom template.

Does QuoteCloud integrate with my CRM and other business tools?

Yes. QuoteCloud connects with CRMs such as Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, Monday and Microsoft Dynamics, plus accounting, payment and productivity tools, so customer data flows straight into your documents without redundant data entry. A GraphQL/REST API covers custom connections.

Can QuoteCloud generate documents automatically at high volume?

Yes. Through the GraphQL/REST API, any application that can make HTTPS requests can exchange document data with a QuoteCloud account, so you can programmatically produce any quantity of documents around the clock for archiving, email marketing, invoicing or other business needs.

How does QuoteCloud reduce errors and support compliance?

Templates enforce consistent structure, approved wording, disclaimers and compliance elements, while access permissions and version control keep everyone working from the right document. That reduces the risk of incorrect wording, omissions or other errors slipping into your contracts and proposals.

Can I add AI-written content and rich media to documents?

Yes. QuoteCloud includes AI text generation to draft and rewrite content, and you can embed videos, spreadsheets, animated GIFs, images and other media, making proposals and quotes more engaging while cutting the time spent writing and assembling them.

What are interactive pricing tables?

Interactive pricing tables let clients toggle options, compare packages and adjust quantities directly inside the document, so pricing is clear and there is less back-and-forth. Clients can then accept and sign online.

Start a free 14-day trial with no credit card required, or request a personalised demo. Ready-made templates help you generate your first document quickly.
