Questions, answered

What collaboration features does QuoteCloud provide?

QuoteCloud offers real-time collaboration, inline commenting and feedback, full version history, granular permission controls, and notifications and alerts. Together they streamline teamwork across quote, proposal and contract creation, so documents stay accurate and move forward faster.

How does real-time collaboration work in QuoteCloud?

Multiple users can edit the same document at the same time and see each other's changes instantly. That means faster iteration and fewer bottlenecks when creating and refining quotes, proposals and agreements.

Can clients and external stakeholders collaborate on documents?

Yes. Invite clients and external stakeholders to review, comment on and sign documents, while permission controls limit what they can access. You collect feedback from buyers and partners without giving up security or control.

How does version history help my team?

QuoteCloud tracks document versions automatically, so you can view previous drafts, compare revisions and restore an earlier version if needed. The history creates an audit trail that reduces errors and supports accountability.

What permission controls are available to protect sensitive documents?

Administrators assign granular roles and permissions to each collaborator, defining who can view, comment, edit, approve or restore versions. Only authorised people can change pricing or contract terms, keeping sensitive information confidential.

How do notifications and alerts keep teams up to date?

QuoteCloud sends alerts when documents are updated, commented on or need action, so team members and stakeholders stay informed and respond faster. Timely notifications cut follow-up overhead and speed up approvals and signatures.

Can collaboration be used in sales quoting and proposal workflows?

Absolutely. Collaboration works alongside templates, rich media, drag-and-drop editing, interactive pricing tables and eSignature-ready documents, so teams produce accurate agreements efficiently and keep creation, review and approval in one place.

Start a free 14-day trial with no credit card required, or book a demo to see collaboration and document workflows in action. The trial lets you test real-time editing, commenting, version history and permission controls with your own team.
