Questions, answered

What is QuoteCloud proposal software?

QuoteCloud is proposal software that helps teams create structured, persuasive business proposals that clearly communicate value, strategy and outcomes. You build the proposal in one editor with rich content and pricing, collaborate with your team, route it through approvals, and send it for your client to review and e-sign online, managing the whole process in one place.

How is QuoteCloud different from creating proposals in Word, Excel and PowerPoint?

Word is designed for traditional text-based documents, and building a proposal across Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDF tools means moving information between separate files and keeping content and pricing up to date by hand. QuoteCloud brings text, pricing tables, spreadsheet-style calculations, charts, images, videos and other sales content together in one interactive document, so proposals are faster to build and stay consistent.

What can I add to a proposal?

More than 18 content types in a single editor: rich text, advanced pricing tables, spreadsheets, images, videos, forms, diagrams and flowcharts, Gantt charts, embedded PDFs, QR codes and eSignatures. Use them for executive summaries, scope of work, methodology, timelines and milestones, supporting visuals and investment summaries without attaching separate files.

Can I build calculations and budgets inside the proposal?

Yes. An integrated spreadsheet editor lets you build calculations, budgets, comparisons and financial models directly inside the proposal, alongside pricing tables, so supporting numbers, pricing and proposal content stay together in one document instead of separate Excel files.

Can my team work on the same proposal and approve it before it is sent?

Yes. Multiple contributors from sales, delivery and technical teams work in the same document in real time, with internal comments and structured feedback. Configurable, multi-step approval workflows control who can publish or send a proposal, and version history lets you compare or restore earlier drafts with a complete audit trail, so clients always receive the correct version.

Can I keep every proposal on-brand and consistent?

Yes. Reusable proposal templates for different deal types give every submission a consistent structure, brand-aligned formatting and approved messaging, and optional content locking keeps the look, feel and key wording under control while reps tailor each proposal to the client. New team members get up to speed faster, too.

Can clients sign online, and will I know when they open the proposal?

Yes. Proposals are delivered as clean, easy-to-navigate interactive documents that work on desktop and mobile, with built-in eSignatures for acceptance. Real-time notifications tell you when a proposal is opened, viewed, commented on or completed, so you can follow up at the right moment.
