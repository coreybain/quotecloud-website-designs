Questions, answered

What is the AI Co-Worker in QuoteCloud?

AI Co-Worker is an AI assistant built into the QuoteCloud document editor. It drafts and refines proposal and quote content such as introductions, summaries, product descriptions and value propositions, and helps turn raw information into ready-made Content Blocks like pricing tables and travel itineraries, so your documents look polished without manual rewrites.

How does AI Co-Worker speed up proposal and quote creation?

Instead of starting from a blank page, you ask AI Co-Worker for a draft and insert it straight into your document, then expand, refine or rewrite sections in place. It works alongside your templates, content library and pricing, so you spend less time on repetitive writing and more time on the deal.

Can AI Co-Worker convert information from other sources, like airfare searches?

Yes. AI Co-Worker can take information from other sources, such as travel airfare searches, and convert it into QuoteCloud Content Blocks, including professionally formatted itinerary blocks and detailed pricing tables, ready to drop into a travel quote or proposal.

Can I control the tone and style of what it writes?

Yes. You can set the tone (for example professional, friendly, concise, persuasive or technical) and give the assistant your own rules, so suggestions match the formal, data-driven or conversational style that works for you and your brand.

Does AI Co-Worker replace my team?

No. It enhances your team rather than replacing it. Your people stay in full control of content and decisions: edit, refine and approve everything before it is sent, and use AI as a starting point or a finishing tool.

Does it work with my templates, content library and pricing?

Yes. AI Co-Worker is embedded directly in QuoteCloud documents and works alongside your templates, content library, pricing tables and workflows, so there is no switching between tools and generated content stays aligned with your products and services.

Can I send, track and e-sign the proposals it helps create?

Yes. QuoteCloud lets you send, track and e-sign proposals and contracts from within the platform, so the whole workflow, from AI-assisted drafting to client acceptance and a signed contract, happens in one place.
