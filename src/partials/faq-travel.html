Questions, answered

What is QuoteCloud for Travel and how does it help travel businesses?

QuoteCloud for Travel is quoting and proposal software for travel agencies, consultants and tour operators. It turns complex booking data into clear, branded itineraries and travel quotes, so consultants create, send and e-sign proposals faster, with fewer errors, and travellers book sooner.

How does QuoteCloud work with Sabre, PowerSuite and Tramada?

Itineraries created in Sabre GDS or in PowerSuite and Tramada mid-office systems open directly in QuoteCloud. Booking data is imported and structured automatically, so consultants never rekey flights, hotels or transfers, and can focus on enhancing the trip.

Can I add images, maps, PDFs and videos to travel quotes and itineraries?

Yes. Add destination photos, maps, links, PDF brochures and promotional videos to any itinerary or quote. Rich media helps travellers picture the trip and makes your proposal stand out.

What are Content Libraries and how do they speed up travel proposals?

Content Libraries store reusable blocks such as destination guides, visa requirements, travel safety information, packing tips, forms and terms and conditions. Consultants drop them into any itinerary, keeping every document consistent, compliant and fast to produce.

Can travellers view their itinerary on their phone?

Yes. Travellers get free access to their itinerary in the TravelDocs mobile app for iOS and Android, with trip details, documents and travel information available anytime, before departure and while travelling.

Can I brand and style my travel quotes and itineraries?

Yes. Apply your agency's logo, colours and layouts, and tailor the structure for corporate, leisure or group travel, so every itinerary looks professional and on-brand.

Can clients accept and sign travel quotes online?

Yes. Clients approve and e-sign quotes and proposals inside the document, with a secure, auditable signature trail, turning a proposal into a confirmed booking faster.

How do I get started? Is there a free trial?

Start a free 14-day trial with no credit card required, or book a demo. If you need help connecting your GDS or mid-office system, the QuoteCloud team provides setup support.
