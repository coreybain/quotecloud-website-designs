Questions, answered

What is the QuoteCloud Photo & Image Editor?

It is a full image editor built into the QuoteCloud document editor, so you can retouch photos without leaving your proposal, quote or contract. Open it from image and gallery blocks, images inside text, shape image fills, page, column and container background images, and video thumbnails, then save the edited image straight back into the document. It is available as the Image Editor add-on.

Which editing tools are included?

The toolbar includes Select, five selection tools (Rectangular Marquee, Elliptical Marquee, Lasso, Polygonal Lasso and Magic Wand), Fill layer, Draw, three erasers (Eraser, Background Eraser and Magic Eraser), Text, Shapes, Add image and Crop, plus a Layers panel and a Filters panel.

Can I crop, rotate and resize images?

Yes. Crop freely or to 1:1, 4:3 or 21:9, rotate 90 degrees either way or straighten with a rotation slider, and set the exact output width and height with the aspect ratio locked or unlocked. The Original button lets you compare your edit with the untouched photo.

What filters and adjustments are available?

One-click presets (Original, Grayscale, Sepia, Invert, Warm, Cool and Noir) plus Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, Hue and Blur sliders. Presets and adjustments combine, each slider can be reset on its own, and Reset all returns the photo to its original look.

Can I remove or erase a background?

Yes, for plain or evenly coloured backgrounds. The Background Eraser and Magic Eraser remove similar colours with an adjustable tolerance, and the Magic Wand and lasso tools let you select an area and delete it. Erased areas become transparent and reveal the layers below, and transparent images are saved as PNG.

Does the image editor support layers?

Yes. Text, shapes, drawings and added images each sit on their own layer. You can rename, show or hide, lock, set opacity, drag to reorder, duplicate and merge layers, add a new blank layer, or move any layer to the background.

Can I add text, shapes and logos to a photo?

Yes. Add text with a choice of fonts and size, bold, italic, alignment and colour controls; draw rectangles, ellipses, lines, arrows, polygons, stars, free-form shapes, speech bubbles and highlights; draw freehand; and place a second image such as a logo or watermark that you can move, resize and rotate.

Can I change an edit later?

Yes. Edits are non-destructive: QuoteCloud keeps the original photo and your layers, crop, rotation and filters, so reopening the editor lets you keep adjusting instead of starting again, without the image losing quality. Undo and redo work throughout, and the saved image is a JPEG for photos or a PNG when it has transparency.
