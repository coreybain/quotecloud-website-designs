Questions, answered

What are Canvas Mode and Grid Mode in QuoteCloud?

They are the two document editing modes in QuoteCloud. Canvas Mode gives you a freeform design canvas where content blocks can be placed anywhere on the page. Grid Mode gives you a structured layout of rows and columns. Choose the mode that best suits the content, the audience and the outcome of each document.

How does Canvas Mode work?

Canvas Mode works like a visual design tool, similar to Canva or PowerPoint. Text, images, price tables, diagrams, shapes, videos, QR codes and other content blocks can be positioned freely, so you can create layered, highly customised layouts that look like a professional presentation, brochure or designed proposal.

What is Canvas Mode best for?

Canvas Mode is ideal when presentation, branding and visual impact matter: highly visual sales proposals, executive presentations, marketing documents, product showcases, destination presentations, designed cover pages, branded client-facing documents, one-page summaries and visual pitch documents.

How does Grid Mode work?

In Grid Mode you build documents from rows and columns. Each row acts as a container: choose how many columns it needs, adjust the width of each column, then place content blocks inside each column and stack them vertically. You get the control of a professional layout system while the document stays easy to edit, organise and maintain.

What is Grid Mode best for?

Grid Mode suits structured, repeatable documents: business proposals, quotes and pricing documents, statements of work, client reports, project plans, travel itineraries, internal documentation, structured sales templates and other repeatable document layouts.

Can I use price tables, diagrams and other content blocks in both modes?

Yes. Both modes are built on QuoteCloud content blocks. In Canvas Mode you position blocks such as text, images, price tables, diagrams, shapes, videos and QR codes anywhere on the page; in Grid Mode you place content blocks inside columns. QuoteCloud offers 18 content blocks, including price tables, spreadsheets, Gantt charts, forms and eSignatures.

Why use QuoteCloud instead of a traditional word processor?

A word processor gives you one rigid document flow. QuoteCloud's dual editing modes give you the best of both worlds: Canvas Mode for freeform design, creative layouts and precise visual positioning, and Grid Mode for structured rows, flexible columns and organised business document layouts, with pricing, interactive content and eSignatures in the same document.
