Questions, answered

Is electronic signature software included with QuoteCloud?

Yes. E-signature functionality is included with every QuoteCloud subscription plan, so you can create quotes, proposals, contracts and agreements and collect electronic signatures in the same platform, without paying for a separate e-signature product.

Can I add a signature or initials anywhere in a document?

Yes. The E-Sign content block can be placed wherever a signature belongs: at the end of a contract, beside an acceptance clause or inside an approval section. You can add several signature locations to one document, and electronic initials wherever a clause, page or section needs to be acknowledged.

Can I electronically sign an existing PDF?

Yes. Upload the PDF and place electronic signatures, initials, text input fields, customer information fields and form elements directly over the original pages. The PDF keeps its original design and becomes an interactive document that is ready to be signed.

Do I need to convert my PDF into a QuoteCloud document first?

No. Where you want to keep the original PDF design, the PDF forms the underlying page content and interactive QuoteCloud elements are positioned over it, so there is no need to rebuild the document in another editor.

Can more than one person sign the same document?

Yes. QuoteCloud supports multi-party signing. Signing requirements can be assigned to several recipients, such as customers, suppliers and internal approvers, and each signer can have more than one signature location in the same document.

How do recipients create their electronic signature?

Recipients can draw their signature, type it or upload an image of it. They complete the whole process electronically, with no printing, signing by hand, scanning or returning paper, and they stay focused on the document you sent.

Can I verify signers and keep a record of the signing?

Yes. Optional recipient verification sends a one-time passcode to the signatory before they sign, and completed documents include a certificate of authenticity. QuoteCloud e-signatures are legally binding and eIDAS compliant, with 256-bit encryption, AWS hosting and an audit trail.

What types of documents can I send for e-signature?

Sales quotes, proposals, contracts, agreements, order forms, acceptance forms and other business documents that need a signature or initials. Pricing, forms, approvals and signatures can all sit in the same document. Start a free 14-day trial, no credit card required.
