Questions, answered

Which booking systems can I import itineraries from?

QuoteCloud imports PNRs and trip files from Sabre GDS and from Tramada, Tres CCTE, PowerSuite and Helloworld's Resworld. Connection requirements depend on your provider, so your QuoteCloud team arranges the integration with you. Once it's configured, booking information comes into QuoteCloud without manual re-entry.

Can I import an itinerary without a GDS integration?

Yes. Right-click the Itinerary block and upload a booking or trip PDF, or paste itinerary data copied from your booking system. QuoteCloud can be configured to recognise the formats you use regularly. It extracts the segments and pricing into a structured itinerary and price table, ready for you to style and enhance.

What can I add to each itinerary segment?

Each segment can carry its own content: a hotel image, a destination video, a travel guide, or a short text block with arrival instructions, local tips or things to bring. You can also attach documents such as e-tickets and tour vouchers to a segment or to the whole itinerary.

How do travellers access their trip in TravelDocs?

Travellers download the free TravelDocs app for iOS or Android, then enter their surname and booking reference. They can view flight and accommodation details and open the documents you attached in QuoteCloud, and the itinerary stays available offline.

Can TravelDocs show my agency's branding?

Yes. TravelDocs automatically shows destination images for each segment. To brand the experience, upload a company banner in your QuoteCloud organisation settings or overlay your logo on the destination images.

What can travellers do in TravelDocs while they're away?

Travellers can check in to flights, check flight status for delays, add their own arrangements, share the trip with family, friends or colleagues, and compare prices with a real-time currency converter. If your account is connected to Sabre GDS, they can also look up alternative flight times and seat availability.

Can corporate travel policies be attached automatically?

Yes. For corporate travel, QuoteCloud can be configured to automatically attach relevant PDFs, such as company travel policies and guidelines, to employee itineraries.

How do I get started?

Start a free 14-day trial with no credit card required, or book a demo. To connect your GDS or mid-office system, the QuoteCloud team will arrange the integration with you.
