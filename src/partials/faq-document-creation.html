Questions, answered

What is online document creation software?

Online document creation software lets you write, design, format and share documents in a web browser. QuoteCloud goes further: one document can combine rich text, images, video, PDFs, spreadsheets, price tables, diagrams, Gantt charts, forms and e-signatures, so a report, proposal or contract becomes an interactive document your client can respond to.

Can QuoteCloud be used as an alternative to Microsoft Word?

For business documents, yes. The Text block gives a familiar word-processing experience with headings, columns, lists, tables, hyperlinks and rich text formatting. The same document can also hold price tables, spreadsheets, diagrams, forms and e-signatures, and your team can edit it together in real time in the browser.

How is QuoteCloud different from design tools like Canva?

QuoteCloud gives you two ways to lay out a document. Canvas Mode lets you place content blocks anywhere on the page, much like a visual design tool, for cover pages, brochures and presentation-style documents. Grid Mode builds structured rows and columns. Both modes work with business features such as interactive pricing, forms, approvals and e-signatures.

Can I create presentations and business reports in QuoteCloud?

Yes. Use Canvas Mode for visual, presentation-style documents such as executive presentations, pitch documents and one-page summaries, and Grid Mode for structured reports, proposals, project plans and client documents. Each one is shared as an online document your audience can open, read and respond to.

Can I add spreadsheets, diagrams and videos to my documents?

Yes. QuoteCloud has 18 content blocks, including Text, Image, Gallery, Video, PDF, Spreadsheet, Price Table, Diagram, Gantt Chart, Forms, eSignature, Shapes, Button, QR Code, Barcode, Table of Contents, Itinerary and Empty Space. Spreadsheets can link to price tables so totals flow between them.

Does QuoteCloud support electronic signatures?

Yes. E-signatures are included in every QuoteCloud plan. Place signature and initial fields anywhere in a document or over an existing PDF, add multiple signers, and let recipients draw, type or upload their signature. Signatures are legally binding and eIDAS compliant, with 256-bit encryption and an audit trail.

Can my team reuse templates and work on documents together?

Yes. Branded templates and themes keep every document on-brand, and personal, team and company content libraries hold approved text, images and pricing to reuse. Several people can edit the same document at once, leave comments, review version history and work within the permissions you set.
