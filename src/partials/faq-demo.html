Is QuoteCloud suitable for my industry?

Yes. QuoteCloud offers industry-specific software add-ons built by experts for sectors such as Travel Agents, Telecoms & Hosting, Importers & Exporters, Events & Hospitality, Legal Services, Transport and Trade services. For example: GDS and mid-office itinerary imports for travel, templates for telco contracts and plans, currency conversion for importers, deposit payments for events, and form and eSignature workflows for legal firms.

How does QuoteCloud handle contracts and eSignatures?

eSignature is built in, so customers can securely sign quotes, proposals and contracts online or on a mobile device. Signing is part of the quoting flow, which turns proposals into signed agreements quickly, reduces manual paperwork and helps shorten your sales cycle.

Can QuoteCloud import travel itineraries from GDS or mid-office systems?

Yes. QuoteCloud can open itineraries exported from your GDS or mid-office system and turn complex booking data into customer-friendly travel documents. It structures the information automatically, so consultants can enrich itineraries with images, maps, videos and other rich content. With the TravelDocs mobile app and content libraries, your itineraries and proposals are professional and mobile-ready.

Does QuoteCloud support pricing automation and CPQ (configure-price-quote)?

Yes. QuoteCloud provides configure-price-quote features and sales quote automation for complex pricing. You can automate pricing based on contract terms (useful for telco plans), apply real-time currency conversion for import and export quotes, and include sector-specific charges such as environmental levies for transport, so every proposal is consistent, transparent and accurate.

Are there ready-made templates and content libraries for proposals and quotes?

Yes. QuoteCloud includes ready-to-use sales proposal templates and content libraries, so teams can reuse approved text blocks, pricing tables, FAQs, terms and conditions and rich media. Templates make quotes and proposals faster and more consistent, while still letting you tailor each one to the client.

Can customers sign quotes and pay deposits on mobile devices?

Yes. Customers can view and eSign quotes from their phone and, in many setups, pay a deposit or the full amount there too. That is especially useful for Events & Hospitality, Trade Services and Travel, where securing a deposit quickly makes winning the business more likely. Mobile signing and payment work together with interactive forms and payment integrations.
