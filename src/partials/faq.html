Questions, answered

What is QuoteCloud?

QuoteCloud is a business document platform. It brings word processing, spreadsheets, configurable pricing, forms, diagrams, video, AI, approvals and eSignatures into one browser-based editor, so teams create proposals, quotes and contracts faster and close deals sooner.

Which documents can I create?

Sales proposals, quotes, estimates, contracts, statements of work, tenders, reports, travel itineraries, forms and any other customer-facing business document.

Can I import my existing documents and PDFs?

Yes. Upload a PDF, split it into pages and overlay QuoteCloud content such as text, images, form fields, signatures and initials, or insert new pages between them. Your original design stays intact and becomes interactive and signable.

Are QuoteCloud eSignatures legally binding?

Yes. QuoteCloud eSignatures are eIDAS compliant, protected with 256-bit encryption and hosted on AWS, with a full audit trail for every signed document.

Can my team edit a document at the same time?

Yes. Multiple people can edit the same document in real time, with changes appearing instantly for everyone, so sales, technical, marketing and management teams can contribute without emailing versions back and forth.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with a free 14-day trial. No credit card is required.
