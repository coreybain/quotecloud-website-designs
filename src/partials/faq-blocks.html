Questions, answered

What are content blocks in QuoteCloud?

Content blocks are the building blocks of QuoteCloud's Multi-Content Document Editor. You combine rich text, pricing, diagrams, forms, project plans, multimedia, eSignatures and interactive content in the same document, instead of switching between Microsoft Word, Google Docs, Excel, PowerPoint, Visio, PDF editors and specialist planning tools.

Which 18 content blocks are available?

Price Table, Text, eSignature, Forms, Gantt Chart, Diagram, Spreadsheet, PDF, Video, Image, Gallery, Button, QR Code, Barcode, Shapes, Table of Contents, Itinerary and Empty Space. Mix and match them in one document to build proposals, quotes, contracts, project plans, itineraries and client presentations.

How does the Price Table block help with accurate quoting?

The Price Table block calculates totals automatically and supports optional items, markups, discounts and pricing summaries. Add ad-hoc line items or select products and services directly from your Product Catalogue, so quotes stay consistent and manual errors are reduced.

Can the Spreadsheet block work with my pricing?

Yes. The Spreadsheet block gives you an embedded spreadsheet for calculations, data tables, financial models, comparisons and resource planning. It can take a total from a price table, run the calculation (for example finance repayments over different terms) and send the results to another price table, all inside the document.

Can clients sign and fill in forms inside the document?

Yes. The eSignature block captures legally binding signatures without leaving QuoteCloud and tracks signing status, and the Forms block collects information, feedback, approvals and requirements from clients, prospects or internal teams directly in the document.

Can I add project plans and diagrams to a proposal?

Yes. The Gantt Chart block shows schedules, milestones, deliverables and dependencies, and the Diagram block creates process flows, organisational charts, solution architectures, mind maps and workflow diagrams, so complex plans are easy to follow without a separate tool.

How does the Itinerary block work?

Create itineraries manually, extract itinerary data from PDF and Word documents, or import bookings directly from Sabre GDS and mid-office platforms including CCTE, Tramada and PowerSuite. Flights, accommodation, transfers, meetings and activities are presented in a clear, structured format.
